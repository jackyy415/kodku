Notes from the Kodku studio

Kodku is a small Hong Kong studio focused on internal software — the systems your team uses every day to dispatch work, track status, and get things done. We ship one scoped, production app in three weeks for a fixed $2,500 USD, then hand it over ready to run.

Why internal apps

Operations move on checklists and timestamps, not slide decks. A good internal app makes the work visible, shortens loops, and reduces copy‑paste error. That’s where we spend all of our studio time.

How the build works

  1. 01 You send the workflow. We write a short scope — no calls required.
  2. 02 We build and show progress. The demo is the app, not a presentation.
  3. 03 Week three: production + handover. Your people can operate it.

Offer is fixed: one production internal app in 3 weeks, $2,500 USD.

What “production” means here

Real usage
Runs for a real team with logins and saved data.
Clear scope
We keep the surface small so the core flow is solid.
Handover
Short runbook, admin access, and an app your staff can run.

If you have an operational problem and a simple workflow, we’ll put it in software and ship it. The product is the app.
